Privacy Policy — Gmail Auto Draft

Last updated: 2026-11-08

  • This application is used only by its developer, for the developer's own Google accounts.
  • Gmail and Google Calendar data is accessed only to create drafts and calendar entries for the developer. Data is processed on the developer's own computer and is not sold or shared with third parties, except that email text may be sent to an AI service to compose draft text.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on the developer's computer and can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
